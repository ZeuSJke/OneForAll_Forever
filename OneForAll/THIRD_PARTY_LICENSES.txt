--- Talents Forever reference data ---
Talent tree structure and beta change metadata are cross-checked against data published by Talents Forever:
https://talentsforever.com/data.json

Talents Forever states that its published talent data is licensed under CC BY 4.0 and may be copied/adapted with attribution.
Attribution: Data from talentsforever.com (https://talentsforever.com)
License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

OneForAll continues to read live talent icons/tooltips/node data from the WoW Forever client at runtime, using the published Talents Forever structure as a reference/fallback for tree layout behavior.

--- QuestieDB / Questie ---
Quest data (givers, turn-ins, prerequisites, chains), NPC/object spawn coordinates
(hunter pet tame locations, recipe sources, dungeon quests and keys, flight masters and
zeppelin masters for Map Landmarks, gear quest rewards with their quest givers and chains for the
Gear Planner in Data/QuestRewards.lua, which reagents vendors sell for the Crafting List in
Data/VendorReagents.lua, class quests with their givers for the trainer window in
Data/ClassQuests.lua, quest givers and turn-ins for the quest icons on the world map in
Data/QuestPins.lua, mobs that drop quest items for the quest marks on nameplates in Data/QuestDrops.lua,
vendors, mobs and objects for the reagents of the profession recipes in Data/ReagentSources.lua,
special quest chains with their givers and rewards for the Armory in Data/SpecialQuests.lua,
the German, French, Spanish and Russian names of quests, NPCs, objects and items and the quest objectives in
Locales/Names_deDE.lua, Names_frFR.lua, Names_esES.lua and Names_ruRU.lua
from its l10n translations)
were generated
from the QuestieDB Forever data, which is derived from Questie.
Sources: https://github.com/Questie/questiedb and https://github.com/Questie/Questie

OneForAll itself is licensed under the GPL-3.0-or-later (see LICENSE.txt).

--- Forever Simple World Map ---
OneForAll includes dungeon map layout data (room picture positions, sizes and
turns, boss and entrance positions) from Forever Simple World Map 0.30.2 by Tarolg
(CurseForge project 1728535), used with the author's
agreement (Data\DungeonMaps.lua, generated by tools/dungeon-maps/fwm_maps.lua).
The room layout of Blackfathom Deeps comes from Forever Simple World Map 0.60.2,
which is published under "All Rights Reserved"; it is used with the author's
agreement (confirmed 08.11.2026).
The room pictures themselves are the game client's own minimap images.
